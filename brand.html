End the Cycle — CBM Australia

Creative leadership for an initiative raising awareness of the cycle of poverty and disability. Working alongside CBM Australia and its partners, we helped shape End the Cycle’s brand, messaging and public presence through photography, video, digital communication and campaign materials.

Deepen App

Brand development and creative production for a digital app helping people connect more deeply with the ideas they encounter. From messages, sermons, and seminars to lectures and conferences, Deepen encourages audiences to engage, reflect and carry their learning beyond the live session.

Stageminded

Establishing a brand that connects performance psychology with the demands of professional dance. Stageminded supports dancers working in companies around the world through counselling and practical mindset training, helping them build confidence, navigate pressure and thrive both on and beyond the stage.

Trish Marks Coaching

Repositioning Marks Consultancy as a personal coaching brand for life and business, resulting in a clearer positioning and a more personal identity for a coaching practice focused on lasting change in life and business.

IGA × Deakin University

Creative development for a supermarket research intervention encouraging healthier food choices. Working with Deakin University and participating Champions IGA supermarkets, we brought healthy eating messages into the shopping environment through vibrant food photography, clear messaging and coordinated in-store design.
